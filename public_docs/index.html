Privacy Policy & Support

1. Privacy Policy

Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. atls is designed with an offline-first and privacy-by-design approach:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register or create an account to use the app.
  • Local Data Storage: All your visited countries, travel logs, dates, notes, and passport information are stored locally on your device using SQLite.
  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not track, collect, transmit, or sell any personal data, location history, or analytics to external servers.
  • Image Export: Exported passport cards and stamped photos are saved directly to your local device photo library only upon your explicit action.
