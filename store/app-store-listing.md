## English

**Name:** atls. Travel Passport
**Subtitle:** Your map, visas and trips
**Promo (≤170):** Paint the countries you've visited on your map, check visa rules at a glance, and plan your next trip.

**Description:**
atls. is your personal world passport, keeping all your travel in one place.

• World map: colour countries you've visited, lived in or want to see. Pinch to zoom, tap any country.
• Visa info: see visa-free, e-visa and visa-required status on every country page (Turkish passport).
• Where this month? Suggestions based on season, visa and places you haven't been.
• Routes: city, food and sightseeing ideas for each country.
• Trip planner: pick dates, keep a checklist and budget, watch the countdown.
• Travel journal: rate each trip, follow your stats and badges.
• Works without an account; sign in to sync across devices.

Visa information is based on Wikipedia data and may change. Check with the embassy before travelling.

**Keywords:** travel,passport,map,visa,trip,planner,journal,countries,holiday,routes
